Portage — Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Portage does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

Speech

When you tap the button, your voice is turned into text by Android’s on-device speech recogniser. The audio does not leave your phone and is not recorded or kept by Portage.

Translation

Translation runs on your phone using Google’s ML Kit on-device models. The text you dictate does not leave your phone.

Screen Access

Portage uses Android’s accessibility service to find the text field that holds the cursor and to insert your translated words into it. It does not read, store or transmit any other screen content.

Clipboard

In apps that refuse direct insertion, your last dictation is placed on the clipboard so you can paste it. This stays on your phone.

Network

Portage connects to the internet only to download language packs from Google. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Children

Portage is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
